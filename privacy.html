Privacy & cookies

This site has no accounts, no sign-up and no tracking of its own. Here is exactly what runs when you open a page, and how to switch the advertising part off.

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Who runs this site

Parking Netherlands is an independent comparison site published by Analytics Ascent, Netherlands. Contact: analyticascent.com. We are not affiliated with any municipality, parking operator or the national parking register.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing that identifies you. There is no login and no form that stores data. The site is static HTML served through Cloudflare; Cloudflare keeps short-lived, aggregated request logs (IP address, user agent, URL) for security and performance under its own privacy policy.

Third parties that load on some pages

Each of these receives your IP address when its resource loads, because that is how the web works. None of them receive anything you type on this site except where stated.

  • Google Analytics counts visits and which parts of the map get used. It runs in Consent Mode: no analytics cookies are set until you accept the consent message; without consent Google receives only cookieless, aggregated pings. How Google uses this data. Opt out any time with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Google AdSense shows the advertisements that fund the site. Google may set cookies and use them to personalise ads across sites. You can decline personalised ads through the consent banner, and you can review Google's use of data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • OpenStreetMap tile server draws the maps. Requests carry your IP and the map area you look at. OSMF privacy policy.
  • Photon (komoot) geocodes the address you type into the parking search. The text you type in that box is sent to photon.komoot.io to be turned into coordinates. It is not stored by us.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces. Google Fonts privacy FAQ.
  • Open Charge Map supplies charger locations on the EV parking guide.
  • Stad Gent open data supplies live occupancy on the Belgium page.

Cookies

We set no cookies of our own. Advertising cookies are set only by Google AdSense and only with your consent where the law requires it. To withdraw consent, use the privacy choices link at the bottom of any page or clear cookies for parkingnetherlands.com in your browser.

Affiliate links

Some pages link to parking booking services. Where a link is an affiliate link it is marked as such next to the link, carries rel="sponsored", and may earn us a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate status never changes what we recommend.

Your rights

Under the GDPR (AVG) you can ask what personal data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, and complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data go to Google via the links above.

Data sources

Parking tariffs come from the Dutch national parking register (NPR), published as CC0 open data; charging point data from NDW / DOT-NL; map data from OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). See About for the full list.
