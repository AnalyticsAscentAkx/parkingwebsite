Parking at Den Haag Centraal - the full picture

Garages around Den Haag Centraal span €2.64 to €4.40 per hour in 2026, and the walk from the cheapest to the closest is short enough that it always pays to check both. The closest garage, Garage Helicon, is about a 3-minute walk at €4.40/hr and €105.60 for a full day.

Cheapest vs closest

If price matters more than a few minutes on foot, P+R Laan v NOI is the value pick at €2.64/hr and €63.36/24hr. For a short visit the convenience of the closest garage usually wins; for a full day the cheaper garages and P+R pull well ahead.

When P+R makes sense

For all-day or multi-day parking, The Hague's Park and Ride is the value play at €2.00 for 24 hours including the ride into the centre. You tap in and out on an OV-chipkaart. That undercuts the central garages once you are staying a full day, and it keeps the car out of the busy centre entirely.
