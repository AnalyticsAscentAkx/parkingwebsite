Parking at Groningen Station - the full picture

Garages around Groningen Station span €1.50 to €2.40 per hour in 2026, and the walk from the cheapest to the closest is short enough that it always pays to check both. The closest garage, Garage Biosparking, is about a 5-minute walk at €2.40/hr and €57.60 for a full day.

Cheapest vs closest

If price matters more than a few minutes on foot, parkeergarage Station Europapark is the value pick at €1.50/hr and €36/24hr. For a short visit the convenience of the closest garage usually wins; for a full day the cheaper garages and P+R pull well ahead.

When P+R makes sense

For all-day or multi-day parking, Groningen's Park and Ride is the value play at €2.00 for 24 hours including the ride into the centre. You tap in and out on an OV-chipkaart. That undercuts the central garages once you are staying a full day, and it keeps the car out of the busy centre entirely.
