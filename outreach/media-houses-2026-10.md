```

Two findings from the national parking register that run against what everyone
assumes, with the data attached:

## 6. electrive.com

**Subject:** Data: the Netherlands passes the EU 1.3 kW/EV test nationally, but 33 to 70 neighbourhoods fail it

```
Dear editorial team,

Two datasets from the Dutch registers that may interest electrive's infrastructure
coverage, both open under CC BY:

1. AFIR per neighbourhood. The Netherlands provides 3.36 kW of public charging power
   per BEV against the regulation's 1.3 kW. Applying the same test to every 2 km
   hexagon, between 33 and 70 neighbourhoods fall below the line depending on the
   walking distance assumed, with the full sensitivity curve published rather than a
   single headline. https://parkingnetherlands.com/charging-gap

2. Price formation. Across 93,002 published tariffs the median is €0.41/kWh, but the
   ten largest networks average between €0.32 and €0.51 for the same electricity.
   Parking under the post adds 27% to 47% to a two-hour stop in city centres.
   https://parkingnetherlands.com/blog/charging-stop-costs

I can supply the per-municipality tables or a 800-word piece in English or German.

Best regards,
[name], Analytics Ascent
