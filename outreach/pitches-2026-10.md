## The story

Two findings, both counterintuitive, both from official tariffs rather than a survey.

**Utrecht is the most expensive Dutch city to park, not Amsterdam.** Median three
hour stay:

| City | 3 hours | 1 hour | 24 hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| Utrecht | €10.74 | €3.58 | €85.92 |
| Haarlem | €10.47 | €3.49 | €83.76 |
| Delft | €9.64 | €3.64 | €72.64 |
| The Hague | €9.60 | €3.20 | €76.80 |
| Amsterdam | €9.00 | €3.00 | €92.00 |
| Rotterdam | €5.50 | €2.00 | €42.50 |
| Eindhoven | €4.50 | €1.50 | €36.00 |

Utrecht costs 2.4 times Eindhoven for the same three hours.

**The gap inside a city is far bigger than the gap between cities.** In Amsterdam the
same three hour stay costs €1.40 at one register-listed garage and €24.00 at another,
a seventeenfold difference, on the same day in the same city. The Hague runs €2.70 to
€13.20, Rotterdam €4.00 to €13.00.

Basis: 323 register-listed garages across 14 cities, 175 with published tariffs.
Published as open data under CC BY 4.0 at parkingnetherlands.com/parking-price-index.
