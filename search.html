Methodology

How this parking search works

Every result comes from the Dutch national parking register (NPR), published as CC0 open data - the same dataset parking apps and municipalities use. For each garage we compute the drive-in cost of your exact stay from the official stepped tariff tables: a 2-hour visit is priced differently from a 26-hour one, and this tool does that arithmetic for all 323 register-listed locations at once (175 publish a tariff; the rest are shown without a price).

Three things to know when reading the numbers. First, prices are drive-in rates - some garages offer cheaper pre-booked online rates, and some apply day caps that the register doesn't encode. Second, distances are straight-line; in a Dutch city grid, actual walking distance runs roughly 20% longer. Third, P+R sites (shown in cyan) often require a public-transport check-in to qualify for the €1-5 day rate - read our P+R guide before choosing one.

If you're comparing cities rather than addresses, start with the city comparison table, or read the 10 rules of Dutch parking to avoid the €82.00 enforcement fine.
