Good to know

Before you drive to Rotterdam Centraal

📅

Free on Sundays

Central Rotterdam does not charge for on-street parking on Sundays. Garages still charge, but the street is free - a real advantage over Amsterdam.

📱

Pre-book and save

Garages like Schouwburgplein and Markthal can be reserved through parking apps for 10-20% off the drive-in rate. See the parking apps guide.

⚠️

Mind the street rate

Centre street parking is €4.24/hr, Monday-Saturday. Miss a payment and it is an €82 fine plus the fee - scan cars patrol constantly.

⚡

EV charging

Several central garages have charging points. Check the EV parking guide for reduced-rate charging bays.

Parking at Rotterdam Centraal - the full picture

Rotterdam Centraal is a far easier place to park than Amsterdam Centraal. Street parking in the centre is €4.24/hr and, crucially, free on Sundays, while the garages around the station span a wide price range that rewards a short walk.

How close can I park?

The nearest garage is Garage Schouwburgplein 2, a two-minute walk from the station at €5.00/hr and €103 for a full day. P+R Proveniersplein sits right beside the north entrance for short connections. If you are willing to walk, the savings are large: Garage Eudokiaplein, about twelve minutes north, charges just €1.40/hr and €33.60 for 24 hours - roughly a third of the price of the closest option.

When P+R makes sense

For all-day or multi-day parking, Rotterdam's Park and Ride is the value play. P+R Kralingse Zoom and P+R Slinge are €2.50 for 24 hours with a fast metro into the centre; you tap in and out on an OV-chipkaart. That undercuts even Eudokiaplein once you are staying a full day, and it keeps the car out of the busy centre entirely.

The Sunday trick

If your visit is flexible, come on a Sunday. Central Rotterdam does not charge for on-street parking on Sundays, so you can park close to the station for free - just watch for the handful of private garages and permit-only stretches that still apply.
