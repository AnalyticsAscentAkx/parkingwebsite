Cheap parking in Amsterdam: what a day trip costs, and the catch

Cheap parking in Amsterdam almost always means leaving the car outside the centre. For a day trip arriving at 10:30 and leaving at 18:00, parking costs anywhere from €6.00 at a P+R to €60.38 on the street in the canal ring. This page prices every option for the same hours, shows the arithmetic, and names the condition each low price depends on.

Last checked 2026-11-09. Sources: national parking register (RDW Open Data) for street and garage tariffs, amsterdam.nl for P+R (as of 2026-10-05).

In one minute. The cheapest option is P+R: €6.00 per 24 hours if you enter after 10:00 and check in and out on public transport with an OV-chipkaart. A bank card or phone does not count, and the fare into town is extra. Without an OV-chipkaart, garages on the edge of the city come next, from €1.40 an hour per the national parking register. Street parking in the centre is €8.05 an hour, every day including Sunday, and around the clock in large parts of the centre; past six hours the €48.30 day ticket is cheaper.

The spread is wide. Paying by the hour on a centre street costs ten times the P+R and nearly six times the Bomengarage. Even the day ticket saves €12.08 over the hourly rate in the centre (€60.38 minus €48.30) and €14.45 in the ring zone (€56.25 minus €41.80).

P+R: the lowest price, with conditions

P+R is park and ride: you park on the edge of the city and continue by metro, tram or bus. The national parking register lists 11 P+R facilities in Amsterdam; Zeeburg alone has several. Tariffs per amsterdam.nl, checked 2026-10-05:

EntryFirst 24 hoursEach further 24 hours
after 10:00€6.00€6.00
before 10:00€13.00€6.00

The P+R rate applies for up to 96 hours. Arriving at 10:30 as in the ranking costs €6.00; entering at 09:30 makes the same day €13.00.

The catch

  • OV-chipkaart or paper GVB card only. The low rate requires checking in and out on public transport with an OV-chipkaart (the Dutch transit smart card) or a paper ticket from GVB, Amsterdam's transport operator.
  • OVpay does not count. Tapping in with a bank card, credit card or phone, known as OVpay, does not qualify for the P+R rate.
  • The fare is extra. The €6.00 covers the parking space only. Metro or tram tickets are paid separately, for everyone in the car.
  • Timing matters. Before 10:00 the first day costs €13.00, so plan a day trip to arrive after it.

Locations with their own page: P+R Sloterdijk, P+R Zeeburg, P+R RAI, P+R Olympisch Stadion, P+R Noord, P+R ArenA, P+R Bos en Lommer and P+R VUmc. Pick the one on your approach road; the rate is the same.

Cheap garages outside the centre

Without an OV-chipkaart, or if you want to skip the P+R rules, park in a garage outside the centre. Per the national parking register the cheapest charge €1.40 to €3.00 an hour, against €8.05 on a centre street. They sit in districts with metro or tram links; each garage page shows the location so you can check the walk to the stop.

Street parking and day tickets

In the centre, tariff zone TC1 covering the canal ring, Dam and Jordaan, street parking costs €8.05 an hour. It is paid every day, Sunday included, and around the clock in large parts of the centre; the sign, the meter or the app gives the exact hours for your street. The ring zone around the centre (TC2) is €7.50 an hour. A day ticket (Dutch: dagkaart) buys a fixed window at a fixed price; the table lists the day tickets per the register. The last column is the ticket price divided by the hourly rate.

Free parking on Sunday, and evenings

Of the 45 paid street zones the register lists for Amsterdam, 16 are free on Sunday, none of them in the centre or the ring around it. The centre zones are not among them: €8.05 an hour still applies there, around the clock in large parts of the centre. Free Sunday parking therefore means a zone outside the centre that you have checked on the street tariff map beforehand.

The centre stays paid in the evening, and in large parts through the night; check the sign or the app for your street. The 19:00 to 24:00 evening ticket costs €32.20 against 5 × €8.05 = €40.25 by the hour; in the ring zone €27.90 against 5 × €7.50 = €37.50. The €6.00 P+R remains cheaper, on the same conditions as in the daytime.

What a fine costs

Not paying, or overstaying, on the street brings a municipal assessment, a naheffingsaanslag. In 2026 it is capped at €82.00 plus one hour at the zone rate: €82.00 + €8.05 = €90.05 in the centre, €82.00 + €7.50 = €89.50 in the ring zone. That is nearly twice the €48.30 day ticket. Scan cars read plates, foreign ones included, and assessments on foreign cars are pursued through debt collectors. Amounts, deadlines and objections: Dutch parking fines.

Checklist

  • For P+R, get an OV-chipkaart or a paper GVB card. OVpay with a bank card or phone does not qualify.
  • Enter the P+R after 10:00: €6.00 instead of €13.00 for the first day.
  • Budget transit fares for everyone in the car; they are not in the P+R price.
  • At a garage, check the day maximum at the entrance or on the garage page.
  • Check your vehicle height: Bomengarage 2.10 m, Stadhuis-Muziektheater 1.80 m.
  • On a centre street for more than six hours between 09:00 and 19:00, buy the €48.30 day ticket.
  • Do not count on free Sunday parking in the centre.
  • For a specific address, price your stay with search.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest parking in Amsterdam?

P+R: €6.00 per 24 hours if you enter after 10:00 and check in and out on public transport with an OV-chipkaart or a paper GVB card. The fare is extra. Without one, garages outside the centre are cheapest, from €1.40 an hour at the Bomengarage in Noord per the national parking register.

How much is P+R if I arrive before 10:00?

The first 24 hours cost €13.00, then €6.00 per 24 hours. The P+R rate applies for up to 96 hours.

Can I use P+R with a bank card or my phone?

No. Checking in with a bank card, credit card or phone (OVpay) does not qualify for the P+R rate. You need an OV-chipkaart or a paper GVB ticket.

Is parking free in Amsterdam on Sunday?

In some zones. 16 of the 45 paid street zones are free on Sunday, none of them in the centre or the ring around it. In the centre you still pay €8.05 an hour, around the clock in large parts of it; the sign or the app gives the exact hours.

When is the centre day ticket worth it?

From six hours. The 09:00 to 19:00 ticket costs €48.30, which equals 6.0 hours at €8.05. For a 10:30 to 18:00 stay it saves €12.08 over the hourly rate.

What happens if I do not pay for street parking?

You receive a municipal assessment (naheffingsaanslag) of €82.00 plus one hour at the zone rate, €90.05 in the centre. Scan cars read foreign plates too.
