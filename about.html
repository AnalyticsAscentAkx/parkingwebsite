About Parking Netherlands

Independent parking intelligence. No paywalls. No hidden agendas. Just the most accurate, useful parking data for the Netherlands.

Last reviewed 29 September 2026

What is Parking Netherlands?

Parking Netherlands is an independent, data-driven platform that helps visitors, expats, and locals find the best parking options across the Netherlands. We compare garages, P+R locations, street parking zones, and free parking across all major Dutch cities.

This is not a static price list. We track rate changes, update data monthly, and provide context that generic search results don't - like transit times, zone rules, local tricks, and the exact strategies that save money.

Who built this?

Parking Netherlands is written and maintained by Analytics Ascent, a small data analytics practice based in the Netherlands. The practice works with web analytics and search data for a living, and its people drive, park and charge in Dutch cities every week. That is why every rate on this site is traced back to the national parking register or a municipal tariff table rather than copied from another guide, and why each page shows when it was last reviewed.

The site started as a private spreadsheet after one too many €40 garage receipts in Amsterdam and a Schiphol long-stay bill that cost more than the flight. Existing parking sites were out of date, behind paywalls, or written by people who had never parked here. So the rates were pulled from the official tariff tables instead, and the spreadsheet became this site.

No parking operator, municipality or app pays for placement. Where a link earns a commission it is marked as such on the privacy and cookies page.

Data sources

We use the following legal, open data sources:

  • Nationaal Parkeer Register (NPR), CC0 open data: garage locations, capacities and the official drive-in tariff tables behind every garage page
  • OpenStreetMap via the Overpass API: parking geometry, P+R sites and entrances
  • Municipal open data portals: Amsterdam, Rotterdam and Den Haag, plus each gemeente's published tariff decision (parkeerverordening) for street zones
  • NDW, the Nationaal Dataportaal Wegverkeer: real-time occupancy and charge-point status feeds
  • OpenChargeMap and operator tariff feeds: EV charger locations and kWh prices

We do not scrape commercial operators (ParkBee, Q-Park, APCOA). We do not bypass APIs. We respect rate limits and attribution requirements.

How often is data updated?

Garage tariffs are re-synced from the NPR feed and each page shows the date it was generated. Street-zone rates are checked when a gemeente publishes a new tariff decision, usually around 1 January. Charger status and prices refresh every half hour from the national feed. Guide pages carry a "last reviewed" date under their title. If something is wrong, tell us and it gets fixed, normally within a few days.

Is this site really free?

Yes. 100% free. No account required. No paywalls. We earn through affiliate links (when you click "Check Availability" and book) and display advertising. Sponsored placement is clearly marked as "Featured".
