Parking at Eindhoven Station - the full picture

Garages around Eindhoven Station span €1.40 to €2.80 per hour in 2026, and the walk from the cheapest to the closest is short enough that it always pays to check both. The closest garage, Garage Kennedy Business Center, is about a 2-minute walk at €2.80/hr and €67.20 for a full day.

Cheapest vs closest

If price matters more than a few minutes on foot, Terrein VideoLab/SX is the value pick at €1.40/hr and €33.60/24hr. For a short visit the convenience of the closest garage usually wins; for a full day the cheaper garages and P+R pull well ahead.

When P+R makes sense

For all-day or multi-day parking, Eindhoven's Park and Ride is the value play at €3.00 for 24 hours including the ride into the centre. You tap in and out on an OV-chipkaart. That undercuts the central garages once you are staying a full day, and it keeps the car out of the busy centre entirely.
