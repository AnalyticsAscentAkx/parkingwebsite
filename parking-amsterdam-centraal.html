Parking at Amsterdam Centraal - the full picture

Amsterdam Centraal sits right in the most expensive parking zone in the Netherlands. Street parking around the station runs at €8.05 per hour, 24 hours a day, seven days a week - there is no Sunday discount and no evening cut-off in the centre. That makes the garages, counter-intuitively, the cheaper on-site choice, and Park and Ride cheaper still.

How close can I actually park?

The nearest garage is Garage Amsterdam Centrum on the Oosterdok, barely 230 metres from the station's east exit - about a three-minute walk. It charges €5.00/hr and €120 for a full day across 434 spaces. On the water side, Garage IJDock is a nine-minute walk west at the same €5.00/hr. If you are happy to walk 12-15 minutes, Piet Heingarage and Garage Markenhoven drop to €4.50/hr (€103/day), the cheapest genuinely walkable garages to Centraal.

Why P+R almost always wins

For anything longer than a couple of hours, the maths is stark. A day in a station garage is about €120. P+R Sloterdijk is €1 for 24 hours and just one train stop - four minutes - from Centraal. Add €6-8 of return transport for two people and the whole trip is under €10. You must tap in and out with an OV-chipkaart to unlock the €1 rate; without it you pay the standard daily tariff. P+R Noord on Metro 52 and P+R Zeeburg on Tram 26 are equally cheap and also feed straight into Centraal.

Is it even worth driving?

For most visitors arriving from elsewhere in the Netherlands, the train beats the car on both time and money. But if you are driving in from abroad or carrying luggage, P+R Sloterdijk followed by a one-stop train is the sweet spot: cheap, fast, and it drops you inside the station you were aiming for. Save the on-site garages for short stops where the €120/day ceiling does not matter.
