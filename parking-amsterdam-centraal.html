The verdict first

Should you park at Centraal at all?

Short answer: only if the €120/day price of convenience is worth it to you.

01

Driving in for a few hours?

The closest garage, Garage Amsterdam Centrum on the Oosterdok, is a 3-minute walk at €5.00/hr. Fine for a short visit; €120 if you leave the car 24 hours.

PARK

Staying the day or overnight?

Take P+R Sloterdijk at €6/24hr and ride the train one stop (4 min) into Centraal. It is one of the best parking deals in Europe for a city this size.

02

Thinking about the street?

Don't. The central zone around Centraal is €8.05/hr, 24/7, including Sundays - more expensive than every garage on this page.

What it actually costs to park at Amsterdam Centraal

The station sits in the priciest parking zone in the country. On the street you are looking at €8.05 an hour, all day, every day, Sundays included. So the garages are the cheaper way to park on-site, and P+R is cheaper again.

The closest garages

Nearest is Garage Amsterdam Centrum on the Oosterdok, three minutes on foot from the east exit: €5/hr, €120 for the day, 434 spaces. IJDock is the same price nine minutes west along the water. Walk 12 to 15 minutes and Piet Hein or Markenhoven come down to €4.50/hr - not a big saving unless you are staying the whole day.

Why locals ride the last stop in

Here is the move most people who know the city make: leave the car at P+R Sloterdijk for €6 a day and take the train one stop into Centraal, about four minutes. Tap in and out with an OV-chipkaart or you lose the €6 rate. Two people, return, is maybe €6 to €8 of transit, so under a tenner all in against €120 to leave the car at the station. Sloterdijk is also one stop the other way to Schiphol. P+R Noord (Metro 52) and Zeeburg (Tram 26) do the same job from the north and east.

Should you drive in at all?

Usually not. Coming from elsewhere in the Netherlands, the train tends to beat the car on both time and money. Driving earns its keep when you are arriving from abroad or hauling luggage, and then Sloterdijk plus a one-stop train is the sweet spot. Keep the on-site garages for the quick stops where €120 a day simply does not come into it.
