How P+R Olympisch Stadion compares in Amsterdam

P+R Olympisch Stadion is a flat-fee facility: the register lists €1.00 whether you stay one hour or 24. Priced garages in Amsterdam charge a median €3.00 per hour and €92.00 per 24 hours, so a whole day here costs less than a single hour in a typical Amsterdam garage. Flat P+R fees usually require a public-transport ticket for the group and a same-day exit; check the signage. The nearest garage with a published hourly tariff is Garage Parnassusweg at 1072 m, €3.50 per hour and €84.00 per day. See Amsterdam in the Netherlands Parking Price Index.

Best for: park and ride: leave the car here for a flat fee and take public transport into the centre.
