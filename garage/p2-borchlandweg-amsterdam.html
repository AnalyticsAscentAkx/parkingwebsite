How Terrein P2 Borchlandweg compares in Amsterdam

Terrein P2 Borchlandweg is a flat-fee facility: the register lists €12.00 whether you stay one hour or 24. Priced garages in Amsterdam charge a median €3.00 per hour and €92.00 per 24 hours, so a whole day here costs about the same as a single hour in a typical Amsterdam garage. Flat fees usually come with a maximum stay; check the signage. The nearest garage with a published hourly tariff is Dome-garage at 413 m, €3.00 per hour and €72.00 per day.
