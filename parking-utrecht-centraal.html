Parking at Utrecht Centraal - the full picture

Utrecht Centraal is the busiest railway station in the Netherlands, and it is ringed by parking - which is good news, because you have real choice. Garages nearby span €2.85 to €6.00 per hour, and unusually the closest garage is not the most expensive.

How close can I park?

The nearest is Garage P3 Radboud on the Jaarbeurs side, a three-minute walk at €3.50/hr and €80 a day. For a fully covered route to the platforms, P5 Vredenburg sits directly under the Hoog Catharijne shopping centre - 1,184 spaces at €6/hr, with an indoor walk of about four minutes. If price matters more than distance, Garage Grifthoek in the north-east is just €2.85/hr (€68.40/day), about an eighteen-minute walk.

When P+R makes sense

For a full day or longer, Utrecht's Park and Ride wins easily. Westraven on the A12 and Papendorp on the A2 are €5 for 24 hours including the bus or tram in - you tap on and off with an OV-chipkaart. That beats every central garage once you are staying all day, and it keeps you out of the tight medieval-centre streets.

Is it worth driving?

Utrecht Centraal is a major national rail hub, so for most trips the train is faster than driving and parking. If you must drive, P3 Radboud for short stays and P+R Westraven for long ones are the two picks that consistently make sense.
